## Built with

Books stands on these open-source projects and open services:

- [Readium Kotlin toolkit](https://github.com/readium/kotlin-toolkit) (BSD 3-Clause) for EPUB rendering
- [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose), [Room](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room), [WorkManager](https://developer.android.com/topic/libraries/architecture/workmanager) and [Navigation](https://developer.android.com/guide/navigation) from Android Jetpack
- [OkHttp](https://square.github.io/okhttp/) for networking and [Coil](https://coil-kt.github.io/coil/) for images
- [kotlinx.serialization](https://github.com/Kotlin/kotlinx.serialization) and [kotlinx.coroutines](https://github.com/Kotlin/kotlinx.coroutines)
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org), whose volunteers have made 75,000+ books free
- [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org), its public API and the scanlation groups credited in every chapter
