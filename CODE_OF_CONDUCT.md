# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Books to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or anything else.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Books.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [Dishan18](https://github.com/Dishan18), privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments or code, or block people from the project, when this code is broken.
